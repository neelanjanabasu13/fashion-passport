## Writing rules

Apply these rules to interface copy, documentation, comments, diagrams, commit-facing prose, submission material, and user communication.

1. Do not use em dashes.
2. Write connected sentences with a natural rhythm instead of staccato fragments.
3. Avoid binary contrast formulas such as "It is not X. It is Y."
4. Begin with useful information instead of throat-clearing phrases.
5. Avoid faux-insight setups.
6. Avoid colon reveals.
7. Avoid dramatic fragments.
8. Support analysis with concrete evidence instead of generic claims about innovation or commitment.
9. Avoid inflated statements about importance.
10. Attribute claims to named evidence instead of vague groups.
11. Use one consistent term for each product concept.
12. End with a concrete conclusion or action instead of a slogan presented as profound.
